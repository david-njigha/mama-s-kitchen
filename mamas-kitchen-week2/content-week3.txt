MAMA'S KITCHEN, WEEK 3: THE LOOK
================================
Week 3 is CSS. The words are already on your pages. This file is
Mama's design: her colours, her fonts, and the classes to add.

Keep working in your OWN Mama's Kitchen folder (it's the one on
GitHub). Copy in styles.css and this file. If your menu.html or
reservations.html isn't finished, copy that page from this pack too.


================================
1. MAMA'S COLOURS
================================

  Dark red            #8b2a1f   (spare, for later)
  Cream               #fff8f0   the page background
  White               #ffffff   cards, the form, the header
  Ink                 #1a1a1a   normal text, the dark panel, the footer
  Grey                #6b7280   paragraphs
  Border              #ece3d4   thin lines around cards and boxes
  Gold                #d4a574   small headings in the footer


================================
2. MAMA'S FONTS
================================
  Headings (h1 to h4):  Playfair Display   (backup: Georgia, serif)
  Everything else:      Inter              (backup: Arial, sans-serif)

  Both are free from Google Fonts: fonts.google.com
  Inter: weights 400 and 600.  Playfair Display: weight 700.
  The <link> Google gives you goes in the <head> of ALL FOUR pages,
  above your own styles.css link.
